### Handling Income
1. you have a three-account system that separates your income,spending and savings;
2. your spending on living costs is no more than 45–50 per cent of your take-home income;
3. your EMI payouts are no more than 25–30 per cent of your take-home income;
4. your savings are at least 15–20 per cent of your take-home income
 
###  Emergency Fund
 1. you have six months’ living costs in an emergency fund;
 2. you are a double-income family with no dependent parents and have three months’ living expenses;
 3. you are a single-income home with dependent parents and have a year’s living costs in an emergency fund;
 4. your emergency fund sits in ultra-short-term or conservative hybrid mutual funds
